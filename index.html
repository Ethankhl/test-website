Production Process

From cell to ship in four controlled steps

Every order follows the same validated workflow — engineered for traceability, documented at every station.

1

R&D & Engineering

BOM review, schematic validation and pilot-run sign-off before any cell enters production.

2

SMT & BMS

Automated pick-and-place populates every protection board; AOI checks every solder joint.

3

Pack Assembly

Cell matching, spot-welding, harness routing and ultrasonic sealing under ESD-safe benches.

4

Aging & QC

48-hour charge/discharge cycling, capacity grading, then serial-level inspection before pack-out.

Our Capabilities

Hand-Built Quality

In-House SMT
